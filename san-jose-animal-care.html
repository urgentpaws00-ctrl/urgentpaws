Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — San Jose Animal Care Center

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from San Jose Animal Care Center?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (Rescue/Transfer: SJACSRescue@sanjoseca.gov | Phone: (408) 794-7297) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from San Jose Animal Care Center?

Yes. San Jose Animal Care Center lists foster-eligible at-risk dogs alongside rescue-only cases. Include the dog's Animal ID (e.g., A123456) when contacting the foster or rescue desk so staff can check availability before a deadline.

How often is this San Jose Animal Care Center urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with San Jose Animal Care Center's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: 2750 Monterey Rd, San Jose, CA 95111.
