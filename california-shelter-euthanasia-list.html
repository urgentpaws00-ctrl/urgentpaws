Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — California Shelter Euthanasia & Red List Dogs

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from California Shelter Euthanasia & Red List Dogs?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (See each dog's card for the direct shelter rescue/foster coordinator email) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from California Shelter Euthanasia & Red List Dogs?

Many urgent dogs on California Shelter Euthanasia & Red List Dogs's board are restricted to approved 501(c)(3) rescue partners (Rescue Pull Only). Individuals who want to foster can apply to foster through a local 501(c)(3) rescue partner and share the dog's Animal ID so the rescue can place a pull hold.

How often is this California Shelter Euthanasia & Red List Dogs urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with California Shelter Euthanasia & Red List Dogs's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: Participating California Municipal Animal Shelters.
