Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — Urgent Pit Bulls & Bully Breeds in California Shelters

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from Urgent Pit Bulls & Bully Breeds in California Shelters?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (See each dog's card for the direct shelter rescue/foster coordinator email) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from Urgent Pit Bulls & Bully Breeds in California Shelters?

Many urgent dogs on Urgent Pit Bulls & Bully Breeds in California Shelters's board are restricted to approved 501(c)(3) rescue partners (Rescue Pull Only). Individuals who want to foster can apply to foster through a local 501(c)(3) rescue partner and share the dog's Animal ID so the rescue can place a pull hold.

How often is this Urgent Pit Bulls & Bully Breeds in California Shelters urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with Urgent Pit Bulls & Bully Breeds in California Shelters's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: Participating California Municipal Animal Shelters.
