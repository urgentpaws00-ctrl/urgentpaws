Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — LA County Animal Care & Control (DACC)

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from LA County Animal Care & Control (DACC)?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (Downey: DACCDowneyRescue@animalcare.lacounty.gov | Baldwin Park: DACCBaldwinParkRescue@animalcare.lacounty.gov | Carson: DACCCarsonRescue@animalcare.lacounty.gov | Lancaster: DACCLancasterRescue@animalcare.lacounty.gov | Palmdale: DACCPalmdaleRescue@animalcare.lacounty.gov | Agoura: DACCAgouraRescue@animalcare.lacounty.gov | Castaic: DACCCastaicRescue@animalcare.lacounty.gov) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from LA County Animal Care & Control (DACC)?

Many urgent dogs on LA County Animal Care & Control (DACC)'s board are restricted to approved 501(c)(3) rescue partners (Rescue Pull Only). Individuals who want to foster can apply to foster through a local 501(c)(3) rescue partner and share the dog's Animal ID so the rescue can place a pull hold.

How often is this LA County Animal Care & Control (DACC) urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with LA County Animal Care & Control (DACC)'s public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued.
