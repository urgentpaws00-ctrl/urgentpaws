Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — Riverside County Animal Services

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from Riverside County Animal Services?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (Rescue/Transfer: transfer@rivco.org | Phone: (951) 358-7387) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from Riverside County Animal Services?

Many urgent dogs on Riverside County Animal Services's board are restricted to approved 501(c)(3) rescue partners (Rescue Pull Only). Individuals who want to foster can apply to foster through a local 501(c)(3) rescue partner and share the dog's Animal ID so the rescue can place a pull hold.

How often is this Riverside County Animal Services urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with Riverside County Animal Services's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: 6851 Van Buren Blvd, Jurupa Valley, CA 92509.
