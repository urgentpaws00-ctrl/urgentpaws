Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — Oakland Animal Services

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from Oakland Animal Services?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (Rescue/Transfer: oastransfers@oaklandca.gov | Foster: oasfoster@oaklandca.gov) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from Oakland Animal Services?

Yes. Oakland Animal Services lists foster-eligible at-risk dogs alongside rescue-only cases. Include the dog's Animal ID (e.g., A123456) when contacting the foster or rescue desk so staff can check availability before a deadline.

How often is this Oakland Animal Services urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with Oakland Animal Services's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: 1101 29th Ave, Oakland, CA 94601.
