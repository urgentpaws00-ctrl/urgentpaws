For Shelters and Rescue Partners

Get Your Urgent and Rescue-Only Dogs Seen Faster

UrgentPaws is a free public directory for California city and county animal shelters. We pull your shelter's urgent deadline, medical, and rescue-preferred dogs into one searchable place for local fosters, adopters, and approved rescue groups, with zero extra work for your staff.

Shelter Systems We Read

  • 24Petconnect Rescue and At-Risk portals
  • Shelterluv and PetHarbor rescue alert lists
  • Public county open-data and map feeds
  • Dedicated shelter /rescue-needed or urgent web pages

Built to Keep Information Accurate

  • Checks every 3 hours to drop dogs that were adopted or pulled
  • Clearly labels Rescue Pull Only dogs so staff get fewer wrong calls
  • Skips animals already safe in foster homes
  • Sends every visitor straight to your official shelter listing

Add Your California Shelter or Update a Listing

If your shelter posts a public urgent, medical, or transfer-preferred dog list that is not on UrgentPaws yet, send us the web link on our contact page.
