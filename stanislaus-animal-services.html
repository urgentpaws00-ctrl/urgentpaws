Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — Stanislaus Animal Services

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from Stanislaus Animal Services?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (Rescue/Transfer: rescue@stancounty.com | Phone: (209) 558-7387) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from Stanislaus Animal Services?

Yes. Stanislaus Animal Services lists foster-eligible at-risk dogs alongside rescue-only cases. Include the dog's Animal ID (e.g., A123456) when contacting the foster or rescue desk so staff can check availability before a deadline.

How often is this Stanislaus Animal Services urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with Stanislaus Animal Services's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: 3647 Cornucopia Way, Modesto, CA 95358.
