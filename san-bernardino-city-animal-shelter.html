Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — San Bernardino City Animal Shelter

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from San Bernardino City Animal Shelter?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (Rescue: sbrescue@sbcity.org | Foster: fosterpet@sbcity.org | Phone: (909) 998-4000) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from San Bernardino City Animal Shelter?

Yes. San Bernardino City Animal Shelter lists foster-eligible at-risk dogs alongside rescue-only cases. Include the dog's Animal ID (e.g., A123456) when contacting the foster or rescue desk so staff can check availability before a deadline.

How often is this San Bernardino City Animal Shelter urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with San Bernardino City Animal Shelter's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: 333 Chandler Place, San Bernardino, CA 92408.
