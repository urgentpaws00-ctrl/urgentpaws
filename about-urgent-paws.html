About UrgentPaws

Helping Urgent California Shelter Dogs Get Seen Before Their Deadline

Every week across California, crowded city and county shelters put dozens of adoptable, injured, and stressed dogs on short deadlines. Because those urgent lists live on separate county websites and rescue portals, fosters, adopters, and rescue groups often find out about a dog with only a few hours left to spare.

Direct Shelter Feeds

Our list updates every 3 hours directly from official shelter urgent boards, including Oakland Animal Services, San Jose Animal Care Center, Contra Costa, Stanislaus, Kern, Riverside, San Bernardino (Devore), and LA Animal Services.

Direct Contact With Shelters

UrgentPaws does not house dogs or collect adoption fees. Every card shows the official Animal ID and links straight to that dog's page on the shelter's own website.

Clear Urgency Labels

We separate routine adoption listings from hard exit deadlines, urgent medical cases, and rescue-only requests so people can focus on the dogs that need help first.

How to Help a Dog on UrgentPaws

  1. Copy the official Animal ID (for example, A1943851) right from the dog's card.
  2. Open the official shelter listing using the button at the bottom of the card to check current kennel hours and status.
  3. Contact the shelter or a local rescue group right away. If a dog is marked Rescue Pull Only, message approved rescue groups with the Animal ID, shelter name, and your offer to foster.
