Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — Devore Animal Shelter

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from Devore Animal Shelter?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (Rescue/Transfer: sbrescue@dph.sbcounty.gov | Phone: (909) 386-9820) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from Devore Animal Shelter?

Many urgent dogs on Devore Animal Shelter's board are restricted to approved 501(c)(3) rescue partners (Rescue Pull Only). Individuals who want to foster can apply to foster through a local 501(c)(3) rescue partner and share the dog's Animal ID so the rescue can place a pull hold.

How often is this Devore Animal Shelter urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with Devore Animal Shelter's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: 19777 Shelter Way, San Bernardino, CA 92407.
