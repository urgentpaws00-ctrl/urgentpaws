Rescue, Foster & Adoption FAQ — Foster-Urgent Shelter Dogs in California

How do 501(c)(3) rescues pull an urgent dog from Foster-Urgent Shelter Dogs in California?

Email the shelter's rescue coordinator (See each dog's card for the direct shelter rescue/foster coordinator email) with your 501(c)(3) rescue organization name, puller contact details, and the dog's exact Animal ID (A#). Always verify pickup windows before sending transport.

Can the public adopt or foster urgent dogs from Foster-Urgent Shelter Dogs in California?

Yes. Foster-Urgent Shelter Dogs in California lists foster-eligible at-risk dogs alongside rescue-only cases. Include the dog's Animal ID (e.g., A123456) when contacting the foster or rescue desk so staff can check availability before a deadline.

How often is this Foster-Urgent Shelter Dogs in California urgent dog tracker updated?

UrgentPaws syncs directly with Foster-Urgent Shelter Dogs in California's public feed every 3 hours and automatically removes dogs whose deadlines have passed or that have been marked adopted or rescued. Shelter location: Participating California Municipal Animal Shelters.
